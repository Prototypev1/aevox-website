FAQ

Questions from the surface.

Can't find yours? Ask us directly.

Is Aevox financial advice?

No. Aevox is an information tool. Alerts, confidence scores, win rates and labels like “likely selling” describe what happened on-chain and what similar patterns looked like before. They aren't predictions or recommendations, and what you do with them is your call.

Do I have to connect my wallet?

Never. Aevox only reads public blockchain data. We don't ask for private keys, seed phrases or wallet access, and we can't touch your funds.

Which chains are supported?

Ethereum and BSC today. Solana wallet tracking is in early access for Premium+ subscribers.

What does “likely selling” mean?

When a wallet sends tokens to an exchange, a sale often follows. Aevox flags that pattern automatically. It's an inference from on-chain behaviour, not a confirmed trade, which is why the label says “likely”.

What is the percentage on an alert?

That's the confidence score: an algorithmic estimate built from historical on-chain patterns. Premium+ shows you how it was calculated. Treat it as context, not a forecast.

Is there a free trial?

The free tier is the trial, and it doesn't expire. Upgrade when you want more history, more tracked wallets or the Premium+ analytics.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Billing runs through Apple or Google, so you cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Do it at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies.

Can I track any address?

Any public wallet, yes. Burn addresses, null addresses and high-volume contracts (liquidity pools, DEX routers, bridges, protocol treasuries) aren't allowed on watchlists. They flood the system with noise and get accounts suspended.

How do I delete my account?

Email privacy@aevox.xyz from the address you signed up with. Your personal data is deleted or anonymised within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy for details.
